GlobalX Privacy Policy

At GlobalX, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your details when you interact with our website, software products, and engineering services.

01 Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to deliver software solutions, respond to business requests, and provide engineering consultation. This includes:

Personal & Contact Details
Full Name, Email Address, Phone Number
Business Information
Company Name, Designation, Website URL
Project Requirements
Tech stack, scopes, specifications, schedules
Direct Communications
Inquiry submissions, feedback, quote forms
02 How We Use Your Information
  • To respond promptly to client inquiries, RFPs, and service requests.
  • To build, deploy, test, and maintain custom software and web applications.
  • To prepare detailed technical proposals, milestone schedules, and quotes.
  • To send critical project deliverables, SLA reports, and maintenance notices.
  • To analyze traffic performance, optimize page responsiveness, and enhance client experience.
03 Cookies & Web Analytics

Our website uses standard essential cookies and browser cache mechanisms to ensure fluid navigation, retain user preferences, and collect anonymous site analytics. Cookies never extract sensitive personal files or unauthorized credentials from your device. You can customize or disable cookies directly through your browser settings.

04 Data Security Protocols

We implement stringent technical safeguards including HTTPS SSL/TLS encryption, secure cloud infrastructure access control, tokenized authentication, and firewall barriers to shield your data from unauthorized access, leakage, or loss.

05 Third-Party Services

We coordinate only with verified, industry-standard third-party providers such as cloud hosting networks, payment gateway APIs, and communication protocols. These third parties access relevant data strictly to execute designated services under contractual confidentiality.

06 Student Academic Projects

Information submitted for academic final year projects, mentorship, or student project guidance is treated with complete confidentiality. We never disclose student identities, project blueprints, or personal information publicly without express student authorization.

07 Your Privacy Rights
Right to request access to stored personal data
Right to request correction of inaccurate records
Right to request deletion or erasure of your records
Right to withdraw consent from promotional notices
08 Policy Updates & Revisions

GlobalX may update this Privacy Policy periodically to align with evolving digital laws and technology standards. Any modifications take effect immediately upon posting to this official page.
